Choose the dictation tool that matches your trade‑off.

Mynah is intentionally narrow: private Mac voice‑to‑cursor, local‑first runtime, one‑time lifetime pricing. It is not trying to be every dictation product at once.

Mynah

Private Mac cursor dictation.

  • $29 / ₹1499 one-time lifetime license
  • Hold Fn, speak, release to paste
  • Core dictation needs no cloud or account
  • Local diagnostics, clipboard controls, transparent stack

Wispr Flow

Cloud-subscription polish and cross-platform reach.

  • Strong fit for AI polish and cross-device sync
  • Subscription-led model based on public positioning
  • Better match for cloud-assisted dictation workflows
  • Different product philosophy from Mynah's local ownership model

Superwhisper

Power-user dictation with broader platform ambitions.

  • Strong local/offline story when configured that way
  • Designed for power users who want many modes and platforms
  • Lifetime pricing is substantially higher than Mynah's launch license
  • Mynah is simpler: a Mac menu-bar voice-to-cursor utility

MacWhisper

Excellent local file transcription.

  • Strong for dragging in audio files and producing transcripts
  • Local-first and one-time Pro pricing — well understood
  • Better fit for files, meetings, subtitles, and exports
  • Mynah is focused on live cursor insertion while you write

Apple Dictation

Free built-in occasional dictation.

  • Good enough for quick built-in use
  • Included with macOS — no extra cost
  • No Mynah-style local diagnostics or clipboard controls
  • Less focused on a menu-bar voice-to-cursor product workflow
