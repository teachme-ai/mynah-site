Support

Permissions Mynah needs.

Microphone

Mynah needs Microphone access to capture your speech for local transcription.

Accessibility

Mynah needs Accessibility permission to place text at the active cursor in other Mac apps.

Applications folder

Run Mynah from /Applications/Mynah.app so macOS permissions attach to the correct app bundle.

If you previously tested SpeakPaste, remove old SpeakPaste permission entries and approve Mynah fresh.
