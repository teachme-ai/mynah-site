Product Roadmap

Current Capabilities & Next Releases

Current Capabilities

Powering your dictation today

Mynah already gives you local Mac voice typing: hold Fn, speak, release, and paste into the app you are using. Transcription runs on local engines, diagnostics stay on-device, and no account or cloud transcription provider is required.

  • Fn-key dictation: Hold, speak, and release to dictate immediately.
  • Local transcription: Runs on-device using private transcription models.
  • Auto-paste at cursor: Drops your words instantly in the active app.
  • Menu-bar runtime: Stays out of sight, always ready in the background.
  • Local captures: Quick access to previous audio clips and transcripts.
  • Local diagnostics: Keep execution logs secure on your own hardware.
  • Mac permission guidance: Native guides to set up permissions reliably.

Coming Next

Local Writing Modes

The next major Mynah release adds local writing modes. Dictate stays verbatim, while Clean Ramble, List, and Prompt help shape spoken thoughts before paste. On supported Macs, Apple’s on-device models may enhance results automatically. When unavailable, Mynah falls back to deterministic local rules.

  • Dictate: exactly what you said
  • Clean Ramble: cleaner sentences from messy speech
  • List: bullets and numbered steps
  • Prompt: structured prompts from rough thoughts
  • Apple on-device enhancement: where available on supported Macs
  • Deterministic fallback: everywhere when Apple Intelligence is unavailable
  • Raw transcript: remains the saved record in local captures

No. The core dictation loop runs 100% locally on your Mac. Audio and transcription are processed on-device using engines like whisper.cpp. No cloud transcription or account is required.
