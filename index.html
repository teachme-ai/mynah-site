Questions

Plain answers.

Is Mynah a subscription?

No. Mynah is a one-time lifetime license for the Mac app.

Is Mynah from the Apple App Store?

No. Mynah is distributed directly from mynah.site. macOS may ask you to approve it on first launch — this is expected for independently distributed apps.

Does Mynah need cloud transcription?

No cloud service is required for the core local dictation loop. Mynah uses whisper.cpp running on your Mac.

Why does Mynah need Accessibility?

macOS requires Accessibility permission for Mynah to place text into the active app at your cursor.

Does Mynah work when the window is closed?

Yes. Mynah is designed as a menu-bar utility. Closing the window does not quit the app — the icon stays in the menu bar and Fn keeps working.

I used SpeakPaste before. Do I need to approve Mynah again?

Yes. Mynah is a new app identity. Remove old SpeakPaste entries from Accessibility in System Settings and approve Mynah fresh.

Accessibility looks enabled but Fn still doesn't work.

macOS can hold a stale entry from an older build. Remove Mynah and SpeakPaste entries from Accessibility, add Mynah again from /Applications/Mynah.app, enable it, and restart Mynah. Restart the Mac if needed.
